Refund Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2025

We want you to be satisfied with share-installs. This policy describes the circumstances under which we offer refunds for our subscription plans.

14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Eligible New paid subscriptions

If you are not satisfied with share-installs, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial subscription payment. This applies to first-time purchases on any paid plan.

Not Eligible Renewal charges

Refunds are not provided for subscription renewal charges. We send a reminder email before each renewal. If you wish to cancel, please do so before the renewal date through your account dashboard.

Not Eligible Partial-month refunds

We do not offer prorated refunds for unused portions of a subscription period when you cancel mid-cycle. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund within the eligible period:

  • Email us at support@share-installs.com with the subject line "Refund Request".
  • Include your account email address and the reason for your request.
  • Refund requests are processed within 5 business days.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Paddle.

Exceptions

We may grant refunds outside the standard policy at our sole discretion in the following cases:

  • A significant service outage or technical failure caused by us that lasted more than 24 continuous hours during your billing period.
  • Duplicate charges due to a billing error on our part.

Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. Cancellation stops future charges but does not automatically trigger a refund. After cancellation, you retain access to the Service until the end of your current paid period.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at support@share-installs.com and we'll be happy to help.
